UsageTracker 0.1.9 adds consent-first provider setup, complete activity history, and a clearer Settings experience.

## Highlights

- Choose which providers UsageTracker may inspect and track through a new provider-by-provider onboarding flow.
- See discovered accounts as they appear instead of waiting for an entire refresh to finish.
- Review complete activity history and all-time totals in the activity grid.
- Navigate separate General and Providers tabs in Settings.
- Get corrected Codex token totals based on recorded account activity.
- Avoid false low-usage warnings while Claude collection is temporarily backing off.

## Installation

The UsageTracker menu bar app requires macOS 14 or newer.

```sh
curl --proto '=https' --tlsv1.2 -fsSL \
  https://github.com/joeymalvinni/usagetracker/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
```

The installer verifies published SHA-256 checksums, archive contents, and ad-hoc code-signature integrity. By default it installs:

- `UsageTracker.app` in `~/Applications`
- `usage` in `~/.local/bin`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This release is ad-hoc signed and is **not notarized by Apple**. macOS may require you to approve UsageTracker manually the first time it opens. Follow the [Open Anyway instructions](https://github.com/joeymalvinni/usagetracker/blob/v0.1.9/docs/troubleshooting.md#opening-the-unnotarized-app) without disabling Gatekeeper globally.
